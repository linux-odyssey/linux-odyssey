How to Make a Sandwich "Properly":

1. Slap your bread awake. It has been loafing around all day!
2. Choose your ingredients carefully. Each one must have its own personality. For example, pick a slice of ham that loves telling jokes.
3. Measure the thickness of each ingredient with a ruler. The margin of error must be no more than 1 mm.
4. Wear white gloves and laboratory goggles while assembling the sandwich to ensure the highest standards of hygiene.
5. Once finished, inspect every inch of the sandwich with a magnifying glass to make sure it is flawless.

Remember: making a sandwich is an exact science. No mistakes or creativity allowed!
